3. Risk aversion

Set how strongly each category affects clue ranking.

Opponents ? Controls the penalty for clues that are semantically related to opponent words. At 0%, opponent similarity is ignored. At 100%, the full opponent risk penalty is applied.

Neutrals ? Controls the penalty for clues that are semantically related to neutral words. At 0%, neutral similarity is ignored. At 100%, the full neutral risk penalty is applied.

Assassin ? Controls the penalty for clues related to the assassin. At 0%, assassin similarity is ignored. At 100%, the full assassin risk penalty is applied. The assassin also has a substantially larger base risk multiplier.
